# Plan

The Plan page builds a 12-month budget from your history and decides where the surplus goes.

## Starting point

Tiles show where you stand today:

- net worth;
- liquid funds;
- typical monthly income;
- typical monthly spending;
- runway today.

## Assumptions

- **History used as baseline**: last 6, 12 or 24 months, or all history.
- **Category detail**: Macro or Detailed.
- **Ignore the current month**: the month in progress is incomplete and skews the average.
- **Income change vs baseline (%)**: applied to every income category.
- **Expense change vs baseline (%)**: applied to every expense category.
- **Plan starts in**: the first month of the plan.
- **Month-to-month pattern**: Historical repeats the shape of your past year; Flat spreads the year evenly.

## Budget

A table with one row per category:

- Baseline / month;
- Baseline / year;
- Adjust %;
- Fixed / year.

You can change a category by a percentage or set a fixed yearly amount.

## Where the surplus goes

Buckets give every euro a job. Each bucket has:

- **Name** and **Kind**: Cash, Invested or Debt;
- **How**: percentage of the surplus or euros per month;
- **Value**: the percentage or monthly amount;
- **Return %/yr**: the assumed annual growth;
- **Already there**: what the bucket holds today.

**Free cash** is the part of the surplus not assigned to any bucket.

**Over-allocated** means the buckets ask for more than the surplus. The cash is unchanged; only the labels do not add up.

## Emergency fund

- **Emergency fund target (months of spending)**: how many months of spending you want to keep.
- **Cover after the plan**: liquid money at the end of the plan ÷ planned monthly spending.
- **Still missing after the plan**: the gap to the target.
- **Months to fill**: how long it takes at the current pace.

Buckets are labels: all surplus and growth stay in your accounts, whatever you call them. The emergency-fund section counts all liquid money, not only the bucket called “Emergency fund”.

## Today vs. end of the plan

A table comparing today and the end of the plan:

- savings rate;
- median monthly spending;
- expense volatility;
- runway;
- net worth;
- liquid funds;
- emergency cover.

## Scenarios

Saved plans are compared side by side:

- a table of all KPIs;
- an **expected path** chart: availability if each plan unfolds exactly as assumed;
- a **volatility** chart for one selected plan, with a select box.

The volatility chart shows:

- a solid median line;
- a dark band for the 25th–75th percentile;
- a light band for the 10th–90th percentile;
- a dotted line for the plan without shocks.

Shocks use the historical monthly net-cash volatility.

## Save, delete and export

- **Save**: saves the current plan under a name. The Simulator will start from it.
- **Delete**: removes the selected plan.
- **Download budget (.csv)**: exports the budget table. Disabled in privacy mode.

## Report

The Plan page can be printed or downloaded as a self-contained HTML report. It includes assumptions, starting point, budget, buckets, emergency fund and KPIs.
