# Dashboard

The Dashboard is the visual overview of your money over time.

## Filters

- **Date range**: the period shown.
- **Time step**: Month, Day, Week or Year.
- **Detail level**: Macro or Detailed.
- **Reserve line**: a horizontal reference line in the net-worth chart. Set it to `0` to hide it.

## What you see

### Balances by account

A line chart of each account's balance over time. Useful for spotting which account grew or shrank.

### Net worth

Net worth over time, with an optional reserve line.

### Income vs. expenses over time

Two series on the same chart, so you can see the gap between what comes in and what goes out.

### Category donuts

- Expenses by category.
- Income by category.

### Active filters

The chip bar shows the date range, time step, detail level and whether amounts are visible or hidden.

## Report

The Dashboard can be printed or downloaded as a self-contained HTML report. The report includes the balances, net worth, income vs. expenses, category donuts and wallet tables.
